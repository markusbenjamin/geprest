You will be presented with a continuous stream of tones through speakers behind the screen. The tones can come from different locations, but your task remains the same: find the longer pauses in-between the tones.

It will be similar to the practice session, only more difficult. There will be way fewer longer pauses. 

In the beginning you must be patient, there will be more typical pauses, so that you can have a good sense of it. 

Keep being attentive, and try to find the very few longer pauses hidden in the stream.

Press the space bar if you hear a longer pause in-between the tones compared to the typical pause duration.

Again, do not drum, hum, or sing quietly during the presentation of the stream.

Get ready.

After clicking 'Start', place your finger on the space bar. The test will start automatically.
