You will be presented with a continuous stream of tones through hidden speakers. In this part, the tones will come only from one location, but your task remains the same: find the longer pauses in-between the tones.

In the beginning you must be patient, there will be more typical pauses, so that you can have a good sense of it. Keep being attentive, and try to find the very few longer pauses hidden in the stream. Be patient, and as fast and as accurate as possible. Don't think, just rely on your instinct.

Again, do not drum, hum, or sing quietly during the presentation of the stream.
