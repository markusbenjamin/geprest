Screener: In this experiment you will see and hear fast changing and occasionally flickering auditory and visual objects, that you need to pay close attention to.

Please confirm that you

- have no history of epilepsy

- have no hearing/visual impairment (wearing glasses or contact lenses are okay)

- have no neurological condition

- are not under the influence of any medication that affects the sensory system and cognitive abilities.



Please do not proceed if any of the items in the list above applies to you.
